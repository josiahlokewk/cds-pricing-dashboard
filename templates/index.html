Hazard curve calibration

Solved by Newton-Raphson root-find, tenor by tenor: the trial hazard rate for each new segment is adjusted until the model's own protection leg exactly matches the market-quoted spread times the risky annuity.

\[ V_{\text{prot}}(\lambda) \;-\; S \times \text{RPV01}(\lambda) \;=\; 0 \]

\[ V_{\text{prot}} = (1-R)\,\frac{\lambda}{\lambda+r}\Big[Z(t_1)Q(t_1) - Z(t_2)Q(t_2)\Big] \]

\[ Q(t) = \exp\!\left(-\int_0^t \lambda(u)\,du\right) \]

Price a contract to see this fill in.

Discount curve calibration

Solved directly, no iteration: the new discount factor appears only once, to the first power, in the OIS swap's own par condition -- given the quoted par rate and every shorter discount factor already solved, plain algebra gives the answer.

\[ C \sum_i Z(t_i)\,\delta_i \;=\; 1 - Z(T) \]
